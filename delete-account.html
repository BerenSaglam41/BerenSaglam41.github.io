Delete Your Futbol Trivia PvP Account

You can permanently delete your account directly from the mobile app.

Steps

  1. Open Futbol Trivia PvP.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Select “Delete Account”.
  4. Follow the confirmation instructions.

Account deletion removes or anonymizes your account information, profile, game progress and associated personal data, except information that must be retained for legal, security or fraud-prevention purposes.

If you cannot access the app, contact us at futboltriviadestek@gmail.com .
