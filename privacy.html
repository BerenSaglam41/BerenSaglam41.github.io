Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Futbol Trivia PvP (“we”, “us”, or “the app”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Username and account information.
  • Email address, if you create or link an account.
  • Device and session identifiers used for authentication and security.
  • Gameplay data such as matches, scores, ratings, friends, rewards and preferences.
  • Basic product events used to improve the app. The current mobile analytics module stores events locally on the device and does not use advertising trackers.

How We Use Information

  • To provide authentication and account recovery.
  • To operate matchmaking, online matches, rankings and social features.
  • To save progress, rewards, preferences and game history.
  • To protect the service against abuse and unauthorized access.
  • To improve reliability and user experience.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that host our database, API and email delivery infrastructure, only as needed to operate the service.

Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete your account from the app by opening Settings, selecting “Delete Account”, and confirming the deletion. Account data and associated personal information are deleted or anonymized as part of this process, except where retention is required for legal, security or fraud-prevention purposes.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
